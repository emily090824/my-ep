    About Emily

    Emily Maranto Hill is a Louisiana-based marketing and communications leader with eight years of experience connecting audience insights, creative strategy, and business results. As Senior Paid Media Specialist at National University, she leads cross-channel media strategy with responsibility for a $40 million annual budget. She also serves as Chief Operations Officer at The Matchbox, overseeing agency operations, team development, and client delivery. Her work spans enterprise campaigns, agency growth, and the systems that help teams do their best work.

    Alongside her professional work, Emily is an author and media studies scholar interested in how people connect with stories and communities online. She holds an M.A. in Lifespan and Digital Communication from Old Dominion University (summa cum laude, 2025). Her master's thesis, Reading the Romance Reader, explored BookTok, romance readership, and the struggle for cultural legitimacy. She also teaches at ODU, where her courses have included Critical Methodologies, Media Audiences, and Media and Society.

    In her free time, she writes stories, snuggles her four fur babies, hunts for her next favorite lamp while antiquing, and dabbles in content creation.

    Novel

    The Blood of a Begonia

    Emily M. Hill · Currently querying

    Set against the manicured world of a Mississippi country club in 1999, this Southern Gothic novel follows a housewife who turns to painting to rescue her family from debt. But when her former muse moves next door with his new wife, artistic renewal gives way to an intimate entanglement. As old desires assume new shapes, the life she’s determined to preserve begins to unravel.
