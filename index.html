    About Emily

    Emily Maranto Hill is a marketing professional, author, and media studies scholar from Louisiana. She studies how audiences gather, talk, and fall in love with stories online — and then puts that knowledge to work.

    She holds an M.A. from Old Dominion University (Summa Cum Laude, 2025), where her thesis examined BookTok and the cultural legitimacy of romance readers, and a Bachelor of Mass Communication from Louisiana State University (Summa Cum Laude, 2021). Today she teaches media audiences and critical methods at ODU. Add a line about your marketing and writing work.
